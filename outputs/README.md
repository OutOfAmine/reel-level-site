# Outputs

Screens and recordings from sites built with this skill. They are unofficial design concepts, shown for illustration. They are not published, approved or endorsed by ONCF or FRMF.

Logos, names and content belong to their owners. These files are not covered by this repository's MIT license and are not offered for reuse.

Departure times, seat maps and tickets in the ONCF concept are demo data. For real travel, use [oncf-voyages.ma](https://www.oncf-voyages.ma/).

## ONCF concept credits

Illustrations were generated with ChatGPT and films with Google Flow (Veo). Mobile screens are from the ONCF Voyages store listing.
